01About

From the kernel to the network

I like to understand how computers work at the lowest level, and then use what I learn to make them harder to break.

Most of my time goes into low level programming, computer networking, and offensive and defensive security. I break systems to understand them, and then I build systems that are harder to break.

I have written a small kernel in C and Assembly, built a honeypot that tracks attackers, and made a SIEM that detects them. I also study malware by writing my own samples and testing them only inside a safe lab.
