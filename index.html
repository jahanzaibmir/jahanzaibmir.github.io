01 About

Engineering trust
from the metal up.

I'm a cybersecurity engineer and researcher who thinks in syscalls, packets and privileges.

My work sits at the intersection of low-level programming, computer networking and offensive & defensive security: breaking systems to understand them, then building ones that don't break.

That has meant writing a kernel in C and Assembly, building honeypots that profile attackers, and a SIEM that catches them. Outside of coursework I study malware by building it from scratch in isolated lab environments, which is the most direct way I know to learn how to defend against it.
